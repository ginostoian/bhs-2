# Link building plan: +50 referring links in 3 months (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027)

**Starting point:** about 50 backlinks (your estimate). **Budget:** time plus ~£100/month. **Rule:** relevant, real, earned links only. No bought links, PBNs, link exchanges at scale or fake reviews. Google discounts or penalises them, and in this niche a manual action would wipe out the whole plan.

**Before you start (30 min):** export current links from Search Console → Links → "Top linking sites" and "Top linked pages". Save them as `docs/seo/backlinks-2026-10.csv`. ## D. Digital PR: 12 links (Month 1–3, ongoing)

This is where the strongest links come from, and the plan uses your best asset: **real London cost data**.

### D1. Journalist request platforms (free tiers): aim for 6 links
Check daily (15 min) and answer anything about renovation costs, extensions, lofts, planning, kitchens, bathrooms, home value or energy upgrades:
- **Qwoted** (free tier)
- **Featured.com** (free tier)
- **#journorequest** on X / Bluesky (search daily)
- **SourceBottle**
- **Help a B2B Writer** (occasional property and business)
- **ResponseSource**: paid. Skip unless budget grows.

Reply template: 2–3 sentences of a genuinely quotable opinion + one number from your own data + name, title, "Better Homes, London builders, 12+ years". Send within 2 hours; speed wins.

### D2. Data-led story: "London Renovation Cost Index 2026" (Month 2): aim for 4–6 links
- Build from your CRM quotes (see the content plan): median cost per m² for extensions, lofts and full renovations, by borough and property type, plus the **renovate vs move** comparison (stamp duty + fees vs extending).
- Publish it as one page `/london-renovation-cost-index` with a clean table, 3 charts and an embeddable image.
- Pitch to: Evening Standard Homes & Property, MyLondon, Homebuilding & Renovating, Ideal Home, Real Homes, House Beautiful UK, Livingetc, The Telegraph property, inews property, local borough papers. Use a short subject line built around a hook: "Extending now costs £X less than moving in Waltham Forest".
- Hook ideas from your data: the cheapest and most expensive boroughs to extend; "a loft adds X% for £Y"; "the £50k extension is dead in Zone 2"; how much costs moved since 2024.

### D3. Seasonal and reactive (Month 3)
- **January "new year, new home" angle:** "The 5 renovations Londoners will ask builders for in 2027", based on your enquiry data.
- **Budget, interest-rate or stamp-duty news:** a quick reactive comment on what it means for "extend or move" decisions, sent the same day.

## Where to point links
Send the anchor text and destination where you can influence them:
- Press and data → `/london-renovation-cost-index` or `/blog/home-renovation-cost-london-2026`
- Suppliers and architects → the specific **project page** (e.g. `/portfolio/ava-e7`)
- Agents and local → `/locations/[area]` (once rebuilt) or the homepage
- Directories → homepage
- Keep anchor text natural: mostly brand ("Better Homes"), some URL, some topical ("London extension builders"). Never exact-match on every link.
