# Backlink plan: 40 real links in 12 weeks (December 2026 to February 2027)

Today Better Homes has about 30 real linking sites. The 131 links from `bhs-2.vercel.app`
were your own site (now redirected), and 116 come from one directory (thomsonlocal).
The sites outranking you have thousands. You can't buy your way there, and you
shouldn't try: a thin profile plus paid links is the fastest way to a penalty.

The good news: you already have the relationships. Your inbox is full of suppliers,
architects, surveyors and specialists you work with every month. Most of the links
below come from them.

**Target: 40 new linking sites by end of February 2027. About 3 to 4 hours a week.**

## 4. Digital PR from your own data: 6 to 10 links (weeks 4 to 12)

You now have something journalists can't get elsewhere: **real London renovation
prices from real jobs** (`/prices` and the house renovation cost guide).

1. **"What London renovations really cost in 2026"**: 20 real jobs, before VAT, by
   area and type. Pitch to local news: Waltham Forest Echo, Hackney Citizen, Islington
   Gazette / Islington Tribune, Ham & High (Hampstead), Enfield Independent, East London
   Advertiser, MyLondon. Angle per paper: "A Walthamstow extension cost £88,900 + VAT.
   Here's where the money went."
2. **Journalist request services** (free tiers): Qwoted, ResponseSource (journalist
   enquiries), #journorequest on X and Bluesky. Reply within an hour with a real number
   from your jobs. Aim for 1 to 2 placements a month.
3. **Seasonal hook (January)**: "The 5 structural costs that surprise London renovators",
   using the Chingford site-visit prices (box frame £14,500, chimneys £10,000, and so on).
